# Code of Conduct

We want Shipvo to be a welcoming place to build and to ask questions.

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Assume good faith, and help newcomers.
- Keep discussion on topic and technical claims honest.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules and may block repeat offenders. Report a problem privately to the maintainers through the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
