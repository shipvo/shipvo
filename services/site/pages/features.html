Features

Your agent builds the app. Shipvo runs it, tests it, records it and ships it.

Shipvo is the mobile platform for the AI era: your agent builds the app, tests it on a phone in your browser, records the demo, and ships it to the stores and to every phone. Parity results against the Android emulator and iOS Simulator are published in the parity results.

Shipvo Phone in your browser

shipvo run android or shipvo run ios shows your app on a simulated Android or iOS phone in a browser tab. No emulator, no Xcode.

Apps, Settings, Browser and updates on the phone

The phone has a Store, a Settings screen, a Browser and OTA update states, all driven by tools your agent can call.

Docs

Lumi tests your app while you watch

npx shipvo lumi opens your app on an Android or iOS phone in your browser. Lumi taps through it, captions each step and lists what broke, with a Replay button. No AI account needed.

Docs

Save a file, the phone reloads

shipvo run android and shipvo run ios rebuild when you save and reload the phone on the same screen. A failed rebuild keeps the last good version and shows the error.
