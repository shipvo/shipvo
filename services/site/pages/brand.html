Space and size

Give the mark room to breathe and keep it large enough that the plane and streaks stay readable.

¼¼¼¼
  • Clear spaceKeep at least a quarter of the mark's width free of text, edges and other graphics on every side.
  • Minimum size24 px on screen, 8 mm in print. Below that, use the favicon artwork.
  • BackgroundsCalm, solid or softly graded. Avoid photographs and patterns behind the mark.
  • MotionThe animated mark is for moments of arrival: a hero, a loading screen. Not as a decorative loop.

Colour

A deep ink for the stage, orange for the thing in motion, sky blue for the agents. Paper white keeps text readable. Click a swatch to copy its value.

On light pages the site switches to warm paper #FAF8F3 with deeper orange #E8780A and blue #0B6FC4 so contrast stays at AA or better. The logo's own edge runs from orange #FF8A1F to blue #5FB8FF.

Use it well

Small things that keep the mark looking like itself.

Do use the mark as it is on light or dark.

Do use the dark symbol for one-colour print.

Do use the light symbol on dark graphics.

Don't stretch or squash it.

Don't recolour the mark.

Don't rotate or tilt it.

Don't put it on busy patterns or photos.

Don't add shadows, outlines or effects.

Don't fade it until it is hard to read.
