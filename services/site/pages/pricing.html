Pricing

Free in the beta. Open source always.

There is no paid Shipvo plan today. The hosted services are free while we are in beta, and everything can run on your own account. Below is what that includes, and what the same jobs cost on Expo's plans so you can compare with real numbers.

Shipvo

Hosted beta

$0
  • Control plane at api.shipvo.dev: signed pointers, cohorts, rollback.
  • Hosted simulator at sim.shipvo.dev, one sandbox per key.
  • Test key with 600 sandbox minutes, valid for 30 days; renew with shipvo login.
  • Up to 3 listed apps per account.

Self-hosted

Your Cloudflare bill
  • Apache-2.0 software, no per-user fee.
  • Control plane on Cloudflare Workers, KV and D1, set up in about ten minutes.
  • The simulator runs on your laptop or any Linux CI runner.
  • We have not measured a typical monthly figure, so we do not quote one. See Cloudflare's pricing.

After the beta

Not decided
  • The software stays open source.
  • Self-hosting stays free of Shipvo fees.
  • If hosted usage is ever charged, it will be announced here and in the changelog first.

Testing every pull request

The other cost is catching bugs before release. If your pipeline builds an iOS binary on EAS for each pull request to test it, that build is usage. The Shipvo simulator needs no binary, and runs on a standard Linux runner.
