A complete Expo app in one command.

shipvo init writes the OTA runtime, the device API, a simulator build, a publish workflow and your signing key. Your screens use one API for camera, location, biometrics, payments and notifications; it is real on phones and simulated for agents, and never pretends otherwise.

One command. Every phone has it.

shipvo publish builds, signs and ships the update; in a GitHub repo the workflow runs it for you on every push. It signs a release pointer (Ed25519), ships bsdiff patches from each installed version, and rolls out by cohort: 1 %, then 10 %, then everyone. Phones verify signature, hash and size, apply without a restart, and discard a release that fails to boot. shipvo rollback --to 7 when you need it.

What the simulator is notIt is not a real device. Payments, biometrics and the camera are simulated and say so. Use it for most of your testing, and a phone for the last mile.
What agents do todayThey test and demonstrate your app. They do not yet buy things for real people through it: that needs OAuth consent, spend limits and a checkout contract, which is on the roadmap, not in the product.
