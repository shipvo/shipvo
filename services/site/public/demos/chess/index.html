Chess on an Android phone and an iPhone

Two simulated phones, one game. Android plays white and iOS plays black, each with its own clock. The game starts by itself. Press "Play against it" to take white.

Moves

    Every square and every piece on these phones has a label, such as "white knight on d5". An assistant reads the board with the typed actions get_board and legal_moves, and plays with move, through @shipvo/mcp. Run it with your own agents.
