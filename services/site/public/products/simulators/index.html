Shipvo Simulators

110 phones on one machine.

Android and iOS simulators that run on your laptop or your CI runner. No cloud, no account, no meter. Your agent opens as many as the machine holds, and every one is its own phone.

How it works

One browser. One phone per session.

Shipvo builds a web copy of your app and serves it to a headless Chromium. Each session gets its own browser context in an Android or iOS phone frame, so a hundred phones cost a hundred small contexts, not a hundred virtual machines.

  1. 01
    Isolated by default

    Every session has its own storage, cookies, network profile and device state. Sessions share nothing.

  2. 02
    The app you ship

    The simulator runs the web build of your app, framed as an Android or an iOS phone with its own screen size and system sheets.

  3. 03
    One API for agents and tests

    Tap, type, scroll, read the screen, set the device, push a notification. Over MCP for agents, or from typed tests in CI.

For agents

    
Your agent spawns its own phones.

    
Add the simulator to Claude Code, Cursor, Codex or any agent that speaks stdio MCP with one command: npx shipvo agents setup. The agent calls open_app to get a phone, list_sessions to see them all and end_session when it is done.

    

      
Free
Apache-2.0 open source. No per-simulator fee, no seat, no minutes to buy.

      
On your machine
The simulator runs on your computer. Your app, your data and your test runs stay there.

      
In your CI
Runs on a standard GitHub-hosted runner, for Android and for iOS.
