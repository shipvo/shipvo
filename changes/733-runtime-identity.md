- `@shipvo/runtime`: new `ensureInstallIdentity(storage, crypto, registerUrl)`, `signReport(identity, body, crypto)`, `enableSignedTelemetry({ storage, registerUrl, crypto? })`, a `webCrypto` default and the `TelemetryStorage`, `TelemetryCrypto` and `InstallIdentity` types. Once an identity exists, telemetry reports carry `install_id` and `sig` (HMAC-SHA256, hex, over the sent body without its trailing `"sig"` member). Without an identity, reports are sent unsigned as before.
- Migration: none. Additive. React Native apps pass their own `crypto` because React Native has no `crypto.subtle`.
