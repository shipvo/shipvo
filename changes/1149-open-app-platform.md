- mcp-tools: `open_app` now sends `platform` (`android` or `ios`) to the simulator as `device.platform`. When the simulator reports the platform of the phone it opened, the result names it, and a different platform returns `platform_mismatch`. A simulator that reports none is not an error. This applies to headless simulator sessions only. If `open_app` attaches to a live `shipvo run` on the other platform, it attaches to that phone and the result says which platform it is. The local and hosted servers share this one schema.
